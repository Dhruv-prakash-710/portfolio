Autonomous Welfare Concierge

AI-Powered Government Scheme Matching

The Problem

Governments offer thousands of welfare schemes aimed at assisting vulnerable populations, but the discovery and application process is notoriously labyrinthine. Citizens must navigate dense, bureaucratic eligibility documents spread across dozens of disjointed portals. Consequently, the people who most desperately need these schemes are often the ones least equipped to find them.

The Beneficiaries

Primary: Citizens from lower socioeconomic backgrounds who may have limited technical literacy or speak regional Indian languages. They can finally bypass bureaucratic friction and instantly discover the financial and social support they are legally entitled to.

Technical Implementation

To solve this, I built a conversational civic-tech platform powered by the Google Gemini API. The architecture centers around a robust Natural Language Processing (NLP) engine capable of understanding complex, unformatted human input in multiple Indian languages.

Instead of forcing users to fill out 50-field forms, the application allows a user to simply speak or type their situation (e.g., "I am a single mother of two living in UP working as a weaver"). The backend engine dynamically parses these entities, cross-references them against a vectorized database of stringent government eligibility criteria, and disambiguates missing data points by asking context-aware follow-up questions.

Outcome & Impact

By translating complex policy documents into an accessible, conversational interface, the Autonomous Welfare Concierge effectively democratizes access to public resources. It reduces the time required to find an applicable scheme from hours of manual research to a matter of seconds, bridging the critical gap between public policy and the citizens it is designed to serve.
