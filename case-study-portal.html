UPTTI CSE Student Portal

Full-Stack Academic Management System

Role

Full-Stack Developer

Client

UPTTI Computer Science Department

Stack

React.jsNode.jsExpressMongoDB

The Problem

Before this project, the Computer Science department at UPTTI relied on a fragmented, manual system of disjointed spreadsheets, emails, and paper records to track student academic data, internships, and cohort performance. This lack of centralization led to data silos, administrative bottlenecks, and an inability for students to easily track their own academic trajectory in real-time.

The Beneficiaries

Primary: Department Faculty and Administrators, who can now monitor entire cohort analytics, approve internships, and manage academic records from a single pane of glass without digging through email threads.

Secondary: CSE Students, who gain a seamless, centralized dashboard to view their grades, upload documents, and track their graduation requirements in real-time.

Technical Implementation

I architected and deployed a highly scalable MERN stack application (MongoDB, Express, React.js, Node.js). To handle sensitive academic data safely, I implemented a robust JSON Web Token (JWT) based authentication system with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), ensuring that students, professors, and admins have appropriately tiered permissions.

The frontend was built with React and styled using Tailwind CSS for a responsive, modern interface. State management was handled meticulously to ensure real-time updates when admins modify records. The backend RESTful API was optimized in Node.js/Express to handle high concurrency, ensuring the portal remains fast even during peak periods like exam result days.

Outcome & Impact

The portal successfully digitized and centralized the department's data infrastructure, effectively eliminating the reliance on manual spreadsheets. It drastically reduced administrative overhead for the faculty and provided a vastly improved, transparent academic experience for the student body.
