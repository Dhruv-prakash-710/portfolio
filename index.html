RetinaX

Automated Diabetic Retinopathy Screening

A comprehensive, AI-driven clinical pipeline engineered to automate the screening and severity grading of Diabetic Retinopathy. The system intelligently pre-processes fundus images using CLAHE for optimal contrast, performs precise lesion segmentation to detect microaneurysms, and classifies disease progression according to the ICDR scale. Crucially, it integrates Grad-CAM heatmaps to provide visual explainability, translating black-box AI into transparent, clinician-trusted insights.
